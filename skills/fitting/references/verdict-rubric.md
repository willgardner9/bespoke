# Verdict rubric

The verdict says how likely the candidate is to be a credible applicant, judged only on evidence in `career.yaml`.

- **strong**: every must-have is `met` or `partial` with evidence, and no `gap` touches the core of the role.
- **stretch**: one or two must-haves are `gap` (or several are only `partial`), but the core of the role is met. Worth applying; the gaps will need handling honestly.
- **weak**: three or more must-haves are `gap`, or any `gap` is at the heart of the role (for example the ad's central tool, qualification or seniority). Say so plainly, name the main gaps, stop and ask whether to continue.

## Judging evidence

- `met`: a fact directly shows it.
- `partial`: related experience exists but is adjacent, smaller or different in kind. Say what is missing in the requirement line.
- `gap`: no fact shows it. Never upgrade a gap because the experience seems plausible for someone like this.

## Weighing

- Treat the ad's "essential" list as must-haves and its "desirable" list as nice-to-haves.
- Seniority mismatches count: a staff-level role against a mid-level history is a gap in scope, not a footnote.
- Do not let nice-to-haves rescue a weak must-have profile.
