# Senior Data Analyst, Northwind Analytics

Northwind Analytics helps independent retailers make better decisions with their data. We are a 120-person company based in Manchester with hybrid working.

## About the role

You will own reporting and experimentation for our retail customers, working closely with product, customer success and finance.

## Essential

- Strong SQL for analysis and reporting
- Python for data analysis
- Building dashboards in Tableau
- Designing and analysing A/B tests
- Experience with dbt for data transformation
- Excellent stakeholder management

## Desirable

- Mentoring junior analysts
- Retail or e-commerce experience

## What we offer

Hybrid working, 28 days holiday, learning budget.
